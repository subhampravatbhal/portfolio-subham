About me

I am Subham Bhal - a Software Developer currently working at Talentelgia Technologies. Over the last 1.6 years in the software industry, I have developed highly performant backend APIs, integrated advanced relational databases, and engineered clean, responsive interface systems.

However, programming is only one dimension of my work. For the past 7 to 8 years, I have cultivated a deep journey as a fine-arts painter. With over 100+ paintings created, I find my greatest inspiration at the intersection where logical systems meet expressive aesthetics.

This dual background allows me to bring a unique perspective to digital creation. I believe software should not just function correctly under the hood—it should look premium, feel responsive, and engage visitors visually.
